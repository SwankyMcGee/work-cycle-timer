Settings reference

Every setting: where it lives, its default, and what it does · App state: November 2026

Timers

SettingWhereDefaultWhat it does
Work lengthTimers10:00 Length of each work cycle (minutes + seconds). Minimum 10 seconds — shorter values snap back to 10 s. Applies right away when the timer isn't running.
Earn lengthTimers5:00 Length of each earn cycle. Same 10-second minimum and rules.
Work cycles per earnTimers1 −/+ stepper (1–10): how many work cycles must finish before the Earn block. The count restarts after each Earn, on Reset, and when the setting changes; Skip → advances the sequence. Adds the Cycle 2 of 3 badge and counts in the bottom line and finish banner.
When time is upTimersPause — wait for Start What happens at the end of a cycle: Pause (banner, waits for a Start hold) · Switch automatically (quiet chime, other timer starts after a moment) · Switch after 10 seconds (countdown in the bottom line, then switches).
Show gentle warnings before time is upTimersOn Master switch for the transition warnings below.
2 minutes leftTimersOff Banner (+ soft chime if sound is on) when 2 minutes remain. Never warns at the very start of a cycle.
1 minute leftTimersOn Same, at 1 minute remaining.
30 seconds leftTimersOn Same, at 30 seconds remaining.

Alerts

SettingWhereDefaultWhat it does
Sound alertsAlertsOn Master switch for all sound (warnings and the end-of-cycle chime). The timer works fully in silence when off.
SoundAlertsSoft chime Which gentle sound plays: Soft chime · Gentle bell · Single ping.
VolumeAlerts50% Loudness of the alert sounds (separate from music volume).
Test buttonAlerts— Plays the chosen sound once, at the current volume.
Soft screen pulse when time is upAlertsOff A slow, gentle fade pulse when a cycle ends — never a hard blink. Leave off for light-sensitivity needs.

Display

SettingWhereDefaultWhat it does
Large text / big displayDisplayOff Enlarges text across the whole app.
Color themeDisplayCalm (soft blue & green) Swatch list of eleven palettes — Calm, Lavender, Mint, Peach, Rose, Ocean, Low contrast, High contrast, Dark, Midnight, Warm dark. Each row shows colour chips (page background, card, text, both dial colours); click to apply instantly; the current one shows a ✓. The sensory animations recolour with the theme.
Timer layoutDisplaySide by side (default) Or One timer at a time — handy on narrow screens and projectors.

Media

SettingWhereDefaultWhat it does
AnimationMediaNone Built-in sensory animation behind the timers: None · Floating bubbles · Drifting soft glow · Slow waves · Gentle rain. No files needed.
Animation reactivityMediaGentle (default) How lively the physics are: Off — plain drifting · Gentle (bubbles bump and occasionally pop; waves swell and crash into foam) · Noticeable — livelier (more bubbles, bouncier, more pops and crashes).
Animation opacityMedia50% How visible the animation is over the page.
Sensory videoMediaNone Pick a background video file. Session only — gone after a refresh; re-pick it.
Video opacityMedia25% How visible the background video is.
Background musicMediaNone Pick a music file. Added music is remembered in the playlist.
Music volumeMedia30% Same setting as the header music-bar slider — moving either moves the other.
Add music filesMedia— Adds files to the playlist; they're saved with your settings (some browsers may block storage for local file copies — just add them again if needed).
Playlist controlsMediaRepeat: Off Drag the dotted handle (or ↑/↓) to reorder · click a name to jump · × removes · Restore removed brings back built-ins · Shuffle randomises · Repeat cycles Off → Playlist → One song.
Open The Ideas EngineIdeas— Opens the suggestion form in a new tab (that click needs internet; the app itself works offline).

Footer (every tab)

SettingWhereDefaultWhat it does
DoneSettings footer—Closes settings.
Reset all settings to defaultsSettings footer— Asks for confirmation, then restores every default. A custom title is kept (identity, not a preference).
Suggest a featureSettings footer— Jumps to the Ideas tab from anywhere in Settings.

Header (not settings — but often confused with them)

ControlWhereDefaultWhat it does
Title pencil ✎Header, beside the titleWork Cycle Timer Click to edit the title; Enter saves, Esc cancels. Saved with your settings.
⏮ ▶ ⏭Header music bar— Hold 1 second each: previous / play-pause / next. Wrap through the playlist; from None, next starts the first track. With Repeat: Off, play restarts from the top after the last track.
Track dropdownHeader music barNone Lists the playlist; picking a track switches to it and plays.
Volume sliderHeader music bar30% Music volume — the same setting as Media → Music volume.
