Troubleshooting

Find your symptom, try the fix · App state: November 2026

I tapped a button and nothing happened

Hold, don't tap. Main and music buttons need a continuous 1-second hold, the gear 3 seconds — keep it pressed until the thin bar fills; release early and nothing fires. Mouse, touch, or hold Enter/Space all work. Deliberate: accidental brushes by students can't trigger anything.

No sound when a warning or time's up arrives

Check in Settings (hold the gear 3 seconds): Alerts → Sound alerts on and Volume not at 0 (press Test); for warnings also Timers → Show gentle warnings before time is up on with your warning ticked. And check the device's own volume/mute.

My settings aren't remembered

Settings are stored in that browser, on that machine only. A different computer, browser or private window starts from defaults; clearing browsing data wipes them. Set it up once per machine you plan to use.

The background video disappeared after a refresh

Expected — picked videos last until the page closes. Re-pick in Settings → Media → Sensory video. Added music is different: it's remembered in the playlist.

Music I added is missing after a refresh

Meant to come back with your settings — but if the browser blocks storage (e.g. a local file copy), just add them again with Add music files. The five built-ins are always under Restore removed.

A cycle shorter than 10 seconds won't save

The minimum is 10 seconds — shorter values quietly snap back. Use 0 min 10 sec as the smallest.

How do I fully reset?

Two different resets: main screen Reset (hold 1 s) only restarts the current cycle and zeroes the work-cycle count; Settings → Reset all settings to defaults restores every setting (it asks first; a custom title is kept).

The sound feels too loud or too quiet

Two separate volumes: alerts — Settings → Alerts → Volume (default 50%; Single ping is the quietest sound); music — the header slider or Media → Music volume (same setting, default 30%). For silence: Sound alerts off + music volume 0 — the timer is designed to work fully silently (see the silent-classroom scenario).

The Ideas Engine button doesn't open anything

Open The Ideas Engine opens the form in a new tab and needs internet — check pop-ups aren't blocked and that you're online. The timer itself never needs the network.

Something seems wrong and it's not on this list

Use The Ideas Engine — or tell whoever looks after the app what you did and what you saw (which browser). Every response is read.
