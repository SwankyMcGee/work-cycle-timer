Full user guide

Organised to match the app's settings tabs · App state: November 2026

Hold, don't tap. Main buttons (Start Work / Start Earn / Pause / Resume, Skip →, Reset) and music buttons: hold 1 second. Settings gear: hold 3 seconds. Let the thin bar fill — release early and nothing happens.

Opening & running a cycle

  1. Open https://swankymcgee.github.io/work-cycle-timer/timer.html — no account, no install. It opens paused at the ready screen: nothing runs until someone deliberately holds Start.
  2. Hold Start Work for 1 second. Status becomes Running, the Work dial fills, the button changes to Pause. (Resume continues it — the dial keeps its place.)
  3. Warnings appear in the banner: 2 minutes left (if enabled), 1 minute left, 30 seconds left — with a soft chime when sound is on.
  4. Time's up: status Time's up, a slow gentle pulse if enabled, and — with the default setting — banner Work is finished! Earn is next. while the timer waits. Hold Start Earn for 1 second; repeat all day.
  5. Skip → (hold 1 s) ends this cycle now and advances the sequence (banner confirms, e.g. Earn is ready to start.); Reset (hold 1 s) returns the current cycle to full length and zeroes the work-cycle count.
  6. Settings: hold the gear ⚙ 3 seconds, pick a tab (Timers · Alerts · Display · Media · Ideas), click Done to close (✕ or Esc also work). Settings are remembered per browser, per machine.

Settings → Timers

  • Cycle lengths — Work length (default 10:00) and Earn length (default 5:00), minutes + seconds. Minimum 10 seconds — shorter values snap back. Applies immediately while not running.
  • Work cycles per earn — −/+ stepper (1–10, default 1). Set 3 for Work → Work → Work → Earn: adds the Cycle 2 of 3 badge (hidden at 1), Next up: Work cycle 2 of 3 in the bottom line, and a counting finish banner (Work cycle 1 of 3 is finished! Work cycle 2 of 3 is next.). Reset zeroes the count · Skip → advances it · changing the setting or finishing an Earn restarts it · every work cycle gets its own full timer, warnings, chime and flash.
  • When time is up — Pause — wait for Start (default): banner, then it waits for a deliberate hold. Switch to the other timer automatically: quiet chime, other timer starts after a moment. Switch after 10 seconds: bottom line counts Starting Earn in 10… → in 1…, then switches; jump early with Start Earn now.
  • Transition warnings — master switch Show gentle warnings before time is up (on), then 2 minutes left (off by default), 1 minute left (on), 30 seconds left (on). Soft banner + quiet chime.
Why pause is the default: nobody is ever rushed into a transition — the single most autism-friendly choice in the app.

Settings → Alerts

  • Sound — Sound alerts on/off (default on); three gentle sounds: Soft chime (default), Gentle bell, Single ping, each with a Test button; Volume slider (default 50%).
  • Screen flash — Soft screen pulse when time is up, off by default: one click adds a slow, gentle fade (never a hard blink) for classes that want a visual cue.
Why: soft chimes instead of beepers — a sharp unexpected beep can be genuinely painful for sensory-sensitive students. The timer also works fully in silence.

Settings → Display

  • Large text / big display — enlarges text everywhere (default off).
  • Color theme — eleven palettes as swatch rows (name + colour chips for page, card, text and both dials). Click a row — applies instantly, ✓ marks the current one. Default Calm (soft blue & green); also Lavender, Mint, Peach, Rose, Ocean, Low contrast, High contrast, Dark, Midnight, Warm dark. Animations recolour with the theme.
  • Timer layout — Side by side (default) or One timer at a time (projectors, narrow screens).

Settings → Media

  • Animation — None (default) · Floating bubbles · Drifting soft glow · Slow waves · Gentle rain; built in, no files needed. Animation reactivity: Off — plain drifting / Gentle (default) (bubbles bump and occasionally pop; waves swell and crash into foam) / Noticeable — livelier. Animation opacity (default 50%).
  • Background media — Sensory video (default None) + Video opacity (25%): videos last until the page is closed. Background music (default None) + Music volume (30%): added music is remembered.
  • Music playlist — Add music files drops tracks into one visible list; drag the dotted handle to reorder (or ↑/↓); click a name to jump; × removes; Restore removed brings back built-ins; Shuffle randomises; Repeat cycles Off → Playlist → One song (Off = play once and stop, Playlist = wrap forever, One song = loop the current track). Order, repeat mode and added files save with settings.
  • Ideas tab — Open The Ideas Engine launches the suggestion form in a new tab (that click needs internet; the app itself works offline). The footer's Suggest a feature jumps here from any tab.
  • Settings footer — Done closes · Reset all settings to defaults asks for confirmation, then restores every default (a custom title is kept) · Suggest a feature → Ideas tab.

Music bar (header, next to the gear)

  • Track dropdown — the whole playlist; picking one switches and plays.
  • ⏮ ▶ ⏭ — hold 1 second each: previous / play-pause / next; they wrap the playlist. From None, next starts the first track and plays. With Repeat: Off, play restarts from the top after the last track. Switching tracks while paused stays paused. Pressing play with no tracks shows No music yet — pick a track in Settings → Media.
  • Volume slider — the same setting as Media → Music volume (default 30%).
  • Five built-in warm-pad loops — Warm pad (gentle chords · slow strings · soft organ · felt piano · breathing pulse). All synthesized from scratch: simple, predictable, instrumental — no copyright concerns for school use.

Title & fun

  • Click the embossed pencil ✎ beside the title to rename the app — Enter saves, Esc cancels (blur also saves). Saved with your settings; Reset-all keeps a custom title (identity, not a preference).
  • Clicking the title five times reveals a hidden scene — click anywhere to go back. Optional; students tend to find it first.

Why everything is gentle

Hold to activate (no accidental triggers) · pause at the end (nobody is rushed) · soft chimes and slow pulses (no beepers, no hard flashing) · plain, predictable motion you can tune or switch off · staff who understand the why are less likely to override these choices — please pass that on.
