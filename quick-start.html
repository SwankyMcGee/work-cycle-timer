Quick start

Everything you need for your first session · App state: November 2026

  1. Open the timer: https://swankymcgee.github.io/work-cycle-timer/timer.html — bookmark it. Any device, any browser; no account.
  2. Check the lengths (once). Hold the gear ⚙ (top-right) for 3 seconds → Timers → set Work length (default 10:00) and Earn length (default 5:00) → click Done. Shortest cycle is 10 seconds.
  3. Hold Start Work for 1 second. A thin bar fills along the button while you hold — let it fill completely. The Work dial starts filling.
  4. Watch the dial. The bright card is running; its semi-circle fills as time passes, so "5 minutes" you can see. The other card stays visible but dimmed, shows its full length, and says Waiting.
  5. Warnings arrive in the banner above the dials — 1 minute left, 30 seconds left — with a soft chime if sound is on.
  6. Time's up: by default the banner says Work is finished! Earn is next. and the timer waits — nobody is rushed. Hold Start Earn for 1 second to begin the Earn block. Then back to Work, all day.
  7. Change anything later: hold the gear 3 seconds, pick a tab on the left (Timers · Alerts · Display · Media · Ideas), click Done to close.

The three main buttons

ButtonWhat it does
Start Work / Start Earn / Pause / Resume The big coloured button — its label always says what a hold will do next.
Skip → Ends this cycle now and advances to whatever comes next in the sequence.
Reset Puts the current cycle back to its full length and, in multi-work mode, zeroes the cycle count.
Hold, don't tap. Every button above needs a continuous 1-second hold — a quick tap does nothing (that's what stops accidental brushes by students). The gear needs 3 seconds. Works with mouse, touch, and by holding Enter or Space.
Why it behaves this way: gentle on purpose. Hold-to-activate prevents accidental triggers; pausing at the end of each cycle means the person using it is never rushed into a transition; the chime is soft, not a beeper.

That's it — you're running. For everything else, see the full user guide or look something up in the settings reference.
