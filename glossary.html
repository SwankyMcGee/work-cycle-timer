Glossary

Every term used in the app and these materials, in plain language · App state: November 2026

Work
The work block — the timer students work against. Default 10:00. Its card is the one that runs during a work cycle.
Earn
The break or reward block that follows work. Default 5:00. The name reflects the idea that the Earn time is earned by finishing the Work block.
Cycle
One timed block — either a Work or an Earn. The app cycles through them all day: Work → Earn → Work → Earn…
Work cycles per earn
A setting (1–10, default 1) for classes that do several work blocks before a break. Set to 3, the sequence is Work → Work → Work → Earn. The Work card then shows a Cycle 2 of 3 badge with pips for progress.
Skip
The Skip → button: ends the current cycle right now and advances to whatever comes next in the sequence — without waiting for the timer. Hold it for 1 second.
Reset
The Reset button: returns the current cycle to its full length and, in multi-work mode, zeroes the work-cycle count (the whole progress starts over). Hold it for 1 second. Not the same as Reset all settings inside Settings, which restores every setting to its default.
Hold to activate
Press a sensitive button and keep it held until a thin bar fills along its bottom — then the action fires. Release early and nothing happens. Main controls and music buttons: 1 second. Settings gear: 3 seconds. Mouse, touch, or hold Enter/Space on the keyboard all work; holding a key does not re-trigger until you release it.
Banner
The strip above the dials that shows messages: transition warnings (1 minute left), finish messages (Work is finished! Earn is next.), and ready messages (Earn is ready to start.).
Pad
The five built-in music tracks — warm, sustained background chords (a "warm pad" sound): Warm pad, Slow strings, Soft organ, Felt piano, Breathing pulse. All are synthesized from scratch, so there are no copyright concerns for school use.
Theme
One of the eleven colour palettes in Settings → Display (Calm, Lavender, Mint, Peach, Rose, Ocean, Low contrast, High contrast, Dark, Midnight, Warm dark). Click a row and the whole app recolours instantly; the current one shows a ✓.
Layout
How the two timers arrange themselves: Side by side (default) or One timer at a time — Settings → Display.
Dial
The semi-circle on each timer card. It fills clockwise as time passes (like a Time Timer's shrinking disk), making "5 minutes" something you can see. The active dial fills; the other shows its full length.
Playlist
The ordered list of music tracks in the music bar: the five built-ins plus any you add. You can reorder, shuffle, remove, restore, and set Repeat (Off → Playlist → One song). Order and repeat mode are saved with your settings.
The Ideas Engine
The suggestion form (Settings → Ideas, or from the home page). One box, no sign-in — staff can request a small change, a new feature, or a whole new tool. Every idea gets read, and new tools appear on the home page.
