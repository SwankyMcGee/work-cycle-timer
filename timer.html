Click a palette — it applies right away and is saved with your settings. The little squares show the page background, card, text and the two dial colors.

Sensory animation (built in)

50%

Calming animations built into the app — no video files needed. They run softly behind the timers and work together with background music. Reactivity adds gentle physics: bubbles bump into each other and sometimes pop, waves swell, sharpen and crash into drifting foam — Off keeps them drifting plainly.

Music playlist

Every track — built-in and added — plays in this order. Drag the dotted handle to reorder (or focus it and press ↑ / ↓), click a name to jump to it, and use × to remove a track.
